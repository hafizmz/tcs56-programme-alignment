## Caveats

1. This is an automated, indicative mapping against IAEA TCS-56 (Rev. 1). It is not an accreditation, MQA or MAHPC registration decision.
2. Mappings use keywords and the course classifications of Appendix A (19 programmes, curricula current in 2026). A course is matched on what its title and synopsis say, not on how it is taught or assessed.
3. Credits were converted to Malaysian CU with factor 0.25 (notional-hours basis, Section 2.1).
4. Professional/clinical items are reported as undergraduate exposure only. TCS-56 expects them in a postgraduate programme, so their absence is not a bachelor's-level gap.
5. Coverage levels (Dedicated, Split, Part of broader) follow Appendix A where an identical course title exists there; otherwise they are inferred from the number of courses and items involved.
6. Numerical Methods and Computational Physics/Programming are one merged item, as in the manuscript.
